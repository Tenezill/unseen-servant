## This application ships no game content

Unseen Servant reads your character data **live from your own Foundry VTT
instance** at runtime. It contains no rulebook, no compendium, no stat blocks,
and no spell, item, monster, or feature descriptions. Whatever rules content you
see in the app is content that already lives in *your* Foundry world, served to
*your* players, and it never leaves your infrastructure.

Test fixtures in this repository use invented placeholder prose for exactly this
reason (see `docs/IP-AUDIT.md`).
